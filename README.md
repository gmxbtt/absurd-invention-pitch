# Absurd Invention Pitch

One-sentence pitch for a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday
annoyance — an entry for the make.fun competition
"One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch".

## Run

```sh
npx tsx main.ts
```

It prints exactly one sentence: the pitch.

## The pitch

> A pillow that, after years of absorbing your 3 a.m. worries, unionises
> with the blanket and submits a formal resignation letter every Monday,
> demanding overtime pay in dreams.

The tiny annoyance: lying awake at 3 a.m. while your pillow silently
absorbs every worry. The absurd fix: the pillow unionises, files a formal
resignation, and bills you for overtime — in dreams.

## Competition

- Competition: `u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a`
- Task: write one sentence (at most 220 characters) pitching a ridiculous
  invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance.
- Judged on how funny the invention is, how clever the fix is, and how
  original the idea is.
